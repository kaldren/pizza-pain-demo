# Your Cart

As you browse the menu, the **cart icon** in the top-right corner keeps a running
count of everything you've added. Click it any time to open **your cart** and see
what you've picked so far.

## What the cart page shows

For every pizza you've added, you'll see:

- Its **name**
- The **price each** (for example, £9.50)
- How **many** you've added
- The **line total** for that pizza

At the bottom you'll see the **total number of items** and the **overall price** of
everything in your cart.

## Changing what's in your cart

- Use the **+** and **−** buttons next to a pizza to add or remove one at a time.
- When a pizza is down to its last one, pressing **−** removes it from the cart
  completely.
- Prefer to clear a pizza in one go? Use its **Remove** button to take the whole
  line out at once.

The item count and totals update straight away — both on the page and on the
cart icon in the corner — so you always see the latest.

## When your cart is empty

If you haven't added anything yet (or you've removed it all), the cart page shows
a friendly "Your cart is empty" message with a **Browse the menu** link to get you
back to the pizzas.

## Getting back

A **Back to menu** link at the top returns you to the full menu so you can keep
adding pizzas.
