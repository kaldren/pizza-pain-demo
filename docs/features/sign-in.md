# Signing In

PAIN Pizza keeps your experience personal and secure by asking you to sign in
before you browse. You sign in with your **Microsoft account** (Microsoft Entra
ID), so there's no new password to create or remember.

## How it works

1. When you open the app, you'll see a **Sign in** button.
2. Click it and you'll be taken to Microsoft's secure sign-in page.
3. Enter your Microsoft work or school account details there.
4. Once Microsoft confirms it's you, you're returned to PAIN Pizza, signed in.

## While you're signed in

- Your name appears in the top-right corner of the app.
- A **Sign out** button sits next to it. Click it whenever you want to end your
  session.

## Good to know

- The whole app is behind sign-in, so you'll always be signed in while using it.
- Your password is only ever entered on Microsoft's own page — PAIN Pizza never
  sees or stores it.
