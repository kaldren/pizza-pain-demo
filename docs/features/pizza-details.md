# Pizza Details & Reviews

When you click **View details** on a pizza from the menu, you land on a page
dedicated to that one pizza — everything you need to decide whether to order it.

## What the page shows

- A large **photo** of the pizza
- Its **name** and **description**
- The **price** (for example, £9.50)
- The full list of **toppings**

## Customer reviews

Below the details you'll find a **Reviews** section, where you can read what other
customers thought of the pizza. Each review shows:

- The reviewer's **name**
- A **star rating** from 1 to 5
- Their **review text**
- The **date** they left it

If a pizza hasn't been reviewed yet, you'll see a friendly "No reviews yet"
message.

## Getting back

A **Back to menu** link returns you to the full menu so you can keep browsing.

## If something's not ready

- While the pizza is loading, you'll see a loading indicator.
- If the pizza can't be found, you'll see a clear "Pizza not found" message.
- If the page can't load, you'll see a friendly error message rather than a
  broken page.
