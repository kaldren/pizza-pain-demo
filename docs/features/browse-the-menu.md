# Browsing the Menu

As soon as you sign in, the home page shows you the full PAIN Pizza menu — every
pizza we offer, laid out as a grid of cards so you can take it all in at a glance.

## What you'll see

Each pizza appears on its own card showing:

- A **photo** of the pizza
- Its **name**
- A short **description**
- The **price** (for example, £9.50)

## Taking a closer look

Every card has a **View details** button. Click it to open that pizza's own page,
where you can see its toppings and read what other customers thought. See
[Pizza Details & Reviews](pizza-details.md).

## If something's not ready

- While the menu is loading, you'll see a loading indicator rather than a blank
  screen.
- If no pizzas are available, you'll see a friendly "No pizzas available" message.
- If the menu can't be loaded, you'll see a clear error message instead of a
  broken page.
